Privacy & cookie policy

What we collect

  • Personal data. We do not require accounts and do not collect names, emails, or payment information on this site.
  • Usage data. Like most websites, we may receive standard technical information (browser type, pages visited) from our hosting provider (Vercel) to keep the site working and secure.
  • Retailer sites. When you click through to a retailer, that retailer's own privacy policy applies from that point on.
  • Contact. If you email us at matheusferreiragu2@gmail.com, we only use your address to reply to your message and do not add it to marketing lists.

Cookies & advertising measurement

We use Google Ads / the Google tag on this site to measure ad conversions (for example, when you click an outbound link to a retailer after arriving from one of our ads) and, where applicable, for remarketing. The Google tag may set cookies or read existing ones for these purposes. You can read how Google uses this information in Google's Privacy & Terms.

We use Google Consent Mode: advertising and analytics cookies stay off until you accept them in the consent banner. You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time in Google Ads Settings or via aboutads.info/choices.

The retailers we link to may set their own cookies once you leave E-Ride Deals. See each retailer's cookie policy for details.
