About DroneDeck

DroneDeck is an independent product discovery and curation platform for drone and RC hobbyists in the United States. We research and organize interesting products, deals, and coupons from partner retailers to help you discover useful parts across the web. We are not a retailer: we don't sell, stock, or ship anything.

Affiliate disclosure (FTC)

DroneDeck is an independent product discovery website. Some links on this site are affiliate links, which means we may earn a commission when you make a qualifying purchase through them, at no additional cost to you.

When you click Check price or View deal, you leave DroneDeck and land on the product's page at the retailer, where payment, checkout, shipping, returns, warranty, and customer service are handled entirely by that retailer. We only curate and present products and deals.

Our selection is independent of our affiliate relationship. Being an affiliate never changes the price you pay — you'll typically pay the same amount as if you visited the retailer directly.

How it works

  1. You discover — browse curated products by category or search for a specific part, brand, or price range.
  2. You compare — see prices, ratings, and specs gathered from retailer listings and compare them side by side.
  3. You check the price — clicking a product takes you to the retailer's own page. Checkout, payment, and delivery all happen there.
  4. We may earn a commission — if you complete a qualifying purchase through our link, the retailer may pay DroneDeck a commission at no additional cost to you.

How we curate

We research and organize products, deals, and coupons from partner retailers into a single catalog so you can discover parts in one place. We do not personally test every product, and we don't create ratings, reviews, or prices — product information comes from the retailer's listing and can change at any time.

When a retailer makes a coupon or promotional offer available, we may feature it here. We only show coupons and discounts shared by retailers or their programs, and we never invent coupon codes, savings, or expiration dates.

What DroneDeck is not

  • We are not an online store and do not sell products.
  • We don't process payments, run checkout, or take orders.
  • We don't handle shipping, returns, or warranty — those are handled by the retailer.
  • We don't invent prices, ratings, availability, or savings.

Privacy policy

DroneDeck is designed to help you discover products, and we keep data collection minimal.

  • Personal data. We do not require accounts and do not collect names, emails, or payment information on this site.
  • Usage data. Like most websites, we may receive standard technical information (such as browser type and pages visited) from our hosting provider to keep the site working and secure.
  • Retailer sites. When you click through to a retailer, that retailer's own privacy policy applies from that point on.
  • Contact. If you email us, we only use your address to reply to your message and don't add it to marketing lists.

Cookie policy

We use only the cookies and local storage needed to make the site work — for example, to remember the catalog between visits so pages load faster. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies of our own.

The retailers we link to may set their own cookies once you leave DroneDeck. See each retailer's cookie policy for details.
