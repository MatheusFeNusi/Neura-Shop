About Electric Deals

Electric Deals is an independent product discovery and curation platform for electric scooter and electric bike riders in the United States. We research and organize interesting products, deals, and coupons from partner retailers to help you discover useful gear across the web. We are not a retailer: we don't sell, stock, or ship anything.

Affiliate disclosure (FTC)

Electric Deals is an independent product discovery website. Some links on this site are affiliate links, which means we may earn a commission when you make a qualifying purchase through them, at no additional cost to you.

When you click Check price or View deal, you leave Electric Deals and land on the product's page at the retailer, where payment, checkout, shipping, returns, warranty, and customer service are handled entirely by that retailer. We only curate and present products and deals.

Our selection is independent of our affiliate relationship. Being an affiliate never changes the price you pay — you'll typically pay the same amount as if you visited the retailer directly.

How it works

  1. You discover — browse curated products by category or search for a specific ride, brand, or price range.
  2. You compare — see prices, ratings, and specs gathered from retailer listings and compare them side by side.
  3. You check the price — clicking a product takes you to the retailer's own page. Checkout, payment, and delivery all happen there.
  4. We may earn a commission — if you complete a qualifying purchase through our link, the retailer may pay Electric Deals a commission at no additional cost to you.

How we curate

We research and organize products, deals, and coupons from partner retailers into a single catalog so you can discover gear in one place. We do not physically test products, and we don't create hands-on reviews. Product information comes from the retailer's listing and can change at any time.

To help you compare models, each product page shows a spec-based score (0–10): an automatic score calculated from the published specifications (motor, battery, tires, load), the current price against the category median, and the buyer ratings published on the retailer's listing. It is not an expert or hands-on rating — every product page states this next to the score.

When a retailer makes a coupon or promotional offer available, we may feature it here. We only show coupons and discounts shared by retailers or their programs, and we never invent coupon codes, prices, savings, or expiration dates.

What Electric Deals is not

  • We are not an online store and do not sell products.
  • We don't process payments, run checkout, or take orders.
  • We don't handle shipping, returns, or warranty — those are handled by the retailer.
  • We don't invent prices, ratings, availability, or savings.

Privacy & cookies (summary)

Electric Deals is designed to help you discover products, and we keep data collection minimal. We do not require accounts and do not collect names, emails, or payment information on this site.

Advertising measurement. We use Google Ads / the Google tag to measure conversions (for example, when you click a link to a retailer after arriving from one of our ads) and, where applicable, for remarketing. Advertising and analytics cookies stay off until you accept them in the consent banner. See how Google uses this data and opt out anytime in Google Ads Settings.
