About GadgetScout

GadgetScout is an affiliate storefront — a curated catalog that points to trusted US retailers. We're not a store: we don't hold inventory, and you don't order from us.

Affiliate disclosure (FTC)

This site is an affiliate storefront. Products shown belong to partner retailers. When you click Buy, you leave this site and land on the product page at the partner store, where payment, shipping and fulfillment are handled entirely by them.

GadgetScout participates in affiliate programs: when you make a purchase through one of our links (a "qualifying purchase"), the partner retailer may pay us a commission. This does not change the price you pay — you'll typically pay the same amount you would by going to the store directly.

From time to time, partners may make exclusive discounts available to our visitors; when we have visibility into those, we pass them along in the price shown.

How it works

  1. You find — search and compare products across categories, prices and ratings.
  2. You tap Buy — you're redirected to the product page on the partner store with an affiliate identifier appended.
  3. Purchase happens at the partner — you pay and receive the item directly from the store; payment, shipping, returns and warranty are their responsibility.
  4. We may earn a commission — if the qualifying purchase is completed, the partner pays a commission, at no extra cost to you.

Privacy

In this demo version, GadgetScout does not collect personal data, uses no tracking cookies and has no membership or checkout of its own. All products and partner stores shown are fictional, created to validate the concept.

Important transparency notice

  • Our role is to point you to offers, not to sell them.
  • Prices, availability and shipping terms are set by the partner and may change at any time.
  • We may earn a commission when you buy through our links.
  • We have no affiliation with any branded marketplace — our name, curation and content are our own.
  • Reviews and ratings shown are illustrative placeholders in this MVP.
