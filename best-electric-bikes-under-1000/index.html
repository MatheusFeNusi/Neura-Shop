What this list does not tell you

  • Prices move. This page was built from prices published on 2026-10-05; a model that crosses the line will move to the other list on the next update.
  • Estimated range is calculated from battery size, not copied from a manufacturer claim. Real range depends on weight, speed, terrain and temperature; when a listing publishes a manufacturer range, we show it on the product page next to our estimate.
  • Speed and power limits for e-scooters and e-bikes vary by state and city in the US — high-power models may be limited to private property or off-road use. Check your local laws before riding on public roads.
  • We have not test-ridden these models. Every figure here comes from the published specification and the current price.
